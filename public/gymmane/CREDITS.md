# GymMane material in LevelUp Life

Based on GymMane by InlitX.

Source revision: https://github.com/InlitX/GymMane/tree/de325c94d4bb5bcc8461bb52754b66494a2c229c

The GymMane stopwatch icon is from `assets/icon/variants/stopwatch.webp`,
included under GPL-3.0-only with GymMane's additional attribution term.

The exercise illustrations in `exercises/` are licensed under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). They come
from Workout Guide by Bryl Lim, Everkinetic, and GymMane by InlitX. See the
[upstream credits](./UPSTREAM_CREDITS.md) for the original source links and
upstream modifications. LevelUp Life wraps the original paths in SVGs and
adds timed frame visibility, a reduced-motion fallback, and a fill color.

Seven catalog entries use GymMane's names and exercise instructions from
`lib/catalog/exercise_catalog.dart` under GPL-3.0-only. See the
[additional terms](./ADDITIONAL_TERMS.md) and the upstream license.

The Progress muscle-map paths are from `lib/catalog/body_svg.dart` (GPL-3.0-only).
The Nunito fonts are distributed under the [SIL Open Font License](./Nunito-OFL.txt).

The full catalog uses the names, instructions, equipment, difficulty, muscle
tags, aliases, and exercise mode metadata from GymMane’s
`lib/catalog/exercise_catalog.dart`. The reference Android APK contains
388 illustration files, all included here. The pinned source revision adds six
more. The sport icons use the regular Phosphor Core 2.1.1 SVGs, as referenced
by GymMane, with a fill-color adaptation. See the
[Phosphor MIT license](./PHOSPHOR-LICENSE.txt).
